Source: `sim/spice/README.md` (S1–S7). Baseline: schematic v0.1 redraw (connectivity identical to `hw/releases/mainboard_v1`).

Verified OK (no change): boost loop margins (PM ≥ 65°, GM ≥ 22 dB), no start-up hiccup, amp inductor peaks ≤ 2.7 A with limiters (5.4 A without the tweeter limiter, so the limiter is mandatory), PVDD_TRIM ripple < 1 µV, strap levels, +3V3 PDN 0.14 Ω vs 0.30 Ω target.

Extra notes from S4–S6: C103 → 2.2 µF also stops EN rising before +3V3 is valid at power-up. The trim curve sits ~0.3 V below the hand table (diode drop at µA); a trim change settles in ~100 ms, so firmware should wait ≥ 150 ms. FFC crosstalk MOSI → CS is 0.69 V with 33 Ω, 0.49 V with 68 Ω, below the 0.99 V V_IL. Electrolytic ESR assumed 0.15 Ω (swept 0.05–0.34 Ω); the TI model dips ~1.6× deeper than the averaged model (EA slew limit).
