## 2. Datasheet layout guidelines (§11.1, p.30), paraphrased

TI says "the basic PCB board layout requires a separation of sensitive signal and power paths" (p.30, §11.1). The checklist is:

1. **Minimise the high-current loop** of switch FET → rectifier FET → output capacitor. This loop carries high di/dt (ns per A) (p.30, §11.1 item 1). In this IC both FETs are internal, so the loop is **SW (7) → VOUT (8) → C_OUT → PGND (6)**.
2. **Minimise the length and area of every SW-pin trace**, and always put a ground plane under the regulator (p.30, §11.1 item 2).
3. Use bulk plus small low-ESR ceramics on the input and the output. **Put the smaller capacitors closest to the IC** (p.30, §11.1 item 3).
4. The ground area near the IC must provide heat-sinking. Connect the wide power buses (VOUT, SW, GND) to large copper areas, or to bottom/inner ground planes **through vias** (p.30, §11.1 item 4).
5. Put the **input capacitor close to VIN and PGND** to reduce input ripple (p.30, §11.1 item 5).
6. Keep the **feedback and compensation network far from the SW trace** (p.30, §11.1 item 6).
7. Use a **separate ground trace** for the FB, COMP, FREQ and ILIMIT components. Join it to power ground at **one point** (p.30, §11.1 item 7). The pin table says the same thing: AGND (pin 2) goes to PGND "via a single point on the printed circuit board" (p.3, §6 Pin Functions).

Related pin-table requirements (p.3, §6):
- VCC (pin 10) needs a ceramic capacitor of **> 1.0 µF** to ground.
- BST (pin 13) needs a capacitor to SW.
- FREQ/SYNC (pin 1) must not float.

## 3. Example layout (Figure 36 "Recommended Layout", p.30, §11.2)

The figure shows a single top-layer view. No layer stack and no vias are drawn, apart from a few round via symbols at RUP, CVCC and VOUT2. Device orientation in the figure:
- The power bars run horizontally across the package, top to bottom: **VOUT, SW, PGND**.
- The top pin row is BST, VIN, EN, VCC, DISDRV (pins 13–9).
- The bottom pin row is FREQ/SYNC, AGND, ILIMIT, COMP, FB (pins 1–5).

What the figure shows:
- **Inductor L** sits to the left of the IC. Its left terminal is on a large **VIN** pour (top-left), and its right terminal connects straight to the **SW bar** on a short, wide neck.
- **C_BST** sits directly above the IC, between the BST pin and the SW copper.
- **C_VCC** sits above the IC next to the VCC pin, with R_EN/EN beside it.
- **C_OUT1** sits immediately right of the IC. Its top pad is on the **VOUT** pour fed from the VOUT bar. Its bottom pad is on the GND pour that continues from the **PGND bar** around the right and bottom of the IC. The output loop is therefore IC bar → cap → back to the PGND bar, with the cap about one package width away, on the top layer.
- The load-disconnect **P-FET** (S, S, S, G / D, D, D, D) sits right of C_OUT1. C_OUT2 is on VOUT2 beyond it, and R_GATE/C_GATE are above.
- The small-signal parts **R_FREQ, R_LIMIT, C_COMP, R_COMP, R_DOWN** sit in a row **directly below** the signal pins (1–5), on a separate local ground island (grey) that ties back to the AGND pin.
- **R_UP** sits beside FB at the lower right. Its top end goes through a via and a hatched trace (drawn as another-layer trace) diagonally under C_OUT1/P-FET to a via on **VOUT2**, the far side of the disconnect FET. In the figure the divider therefore senses the downstream output, routed on another layer away from SW.
- **No input ceramic capacitor is drawn** in Fig. 36, even though rule 5 asks for one at VIN/PGND. The figure is incomplete on this point, so place C_IN using rule 5.

## 4. Thermal pad, vias and land pattern

- **No separate exposed pad.** The three bars (6 PGND, 7 SW, 8 VOUT) are the heat path. RθJC(bot) is 0.9 °C/W and RθJB is 14.5 °C/W (p.4, §7.4). RθJA is 60.2 °C/W on the JEDEC board (p.4, §7.4). The table header says "RNR (QFN Package)", which is a datasheet typo for RNW.
- **Land pattern RNW0013A** (p.38, "Example Board Layout"):
  - Signal pads 1–5 and 9–13: **0.7 × 0.25 mm at 0.5 mm pitch**, non-solder-mask-defined.
  - Bars 6–8: **0.25 mm wide × 3.4 mm long at 0.55 mm pitch** (so the **copper gap between bars is 0.30 mm**). The bars are **solder-mask-defined**.
  - Pad-row centre span is (3.2) mm.
- Note 3 (p.38): the package is designed to be soldered to a thermal pad on the board; see TI SLUA271.
- Note 4 (p.38): **vias under paste should be filled, plugged or tented.**
- **Stencil** (p.39): 0.125 mm stencil. Each bar is split into **3 apertures (1 mm long, 1.2 mm pitch)**, giving **87 % printed coverage** on pads 6–8.
- No via count or size is specified anywhere in SLVSDA7E. Rule 4 only says "using vias".

## 6. Spec cross-check (spec_power.md §6 "Boost (U901)" and §4.1.2)

- "The bars are 0.55 mm apart": this is the **centre pitch**; the copper gap is 0.30 mm (p.38). See the C914 placement note above.
- Custom footprint §4.1.2: pads 0.70 × 0.25 at 0.5 pitch and bars 0.25 × 3.40 at 0.55 pitch **match** p.38.
- §4.1.2 says the "TI stencil ≈ 90 %". The datasheet says **87 %** (p.39). This is a minor difference.
- "AGND (2) connects to PGND only at the pin-6 pad", "FB divider within 3 mm of pin 5" and "COMP parts next to pin 4" are consistent with rules 6 and 7. The 3 mm and 5 mm figures are project targets, not TI values.
- "Up to ≈ 2 W of IC loss at 40 W" is a project estimate. The datasheet gives no layout-specific RθJA.
