## 2. Datasheet layout guidelines (§12.1, p.40), paraphrased

TI says the most critical feature is the **input-cap / power-ground loop** (Figure 12-1, "Current Loop With Fast Edges", p.40). Its traces must be wide and short, with minimum loop area (p.40, §12.1).

0. TI **recommends a 2-layer board with 2 oz copper top and bottom** (p.40, §12.1). This board is 4-layer, which gives better shielding but probably 1 oz outer copper, so thermal performance will differ from TI's characterisation.
1. **IC, inductor, input caps and output caps all on the same layer** (p.40, bullet 1).
2. **Input and output caps as close as possible to the IC.** Make VIN and GND as wide as possible, with **sufficient vias** for low impedance and heat (bullet 2).
3. A **0.1 µF ceramic as close as possible to VIN and GND**, which TI calls "key to EMI reduction" (bullet 3).
4. **SW trace physically short and wide** to minimise radiated emissions (bullet 4).
5. **BST cap and resistor close to BST and the SW node.** Use a **> 10 mil (0.254 mm)** trace (bullet 5).
6. **FB divider as close as possible to FB.** Use a > 10 mil trace (for heat). Connect a **separate VOUT trace** to the upper FB resistor. Keep the FB loop **away from the switching trace**, preferably with a ground shield (bullet 6).
7. **SS cap and RT resistor close to the IC**, with minimal trace length, > 10 mil (bullet 7).
8. Pin table: VIN bypass caps "must be directly connected to this pin and GND". The **GND path to C_IN must be as short as possible** (p.3, Table 7-1, VIN and GND rows).

## 3. Example layout (Figure 12-2 top, Figure 12-3 bottom, p.41, §12.2)

Reference designators follow the Figure 10-1 schematic (p.28): C1, C2 = 10 µF VIN; C3 = 100 nF VIN; C4 = 33 nF SS; C5 = 100 nF BST; R4 = 0 Ω BST; L1 = 6.8 µH; C6, C7 = 22 µF VOUT; C8 = 100 nF VOUT; R6/R7 FB divider; C9 CFF (DNP); R5 49.9 Ω; R1 EN pull.

**Top (Fig. 12-2):**
- U1 sits near the top centre, pins vertical.
- **VIN** enters from the left on a wide pour. **C1 and C2** (VIN bulk) sit just left of U1, and **C3 (0.1 µF)** sits between them and U1, **right next to the VIN/GND pins**.
- The **GND pour** runs below C1–C3 and U1. It carries a **dense via field**: about 40+ vias in a block under and left of the IC, plus a vertical column of about 3 wide running down past the output caps.
- **SW** comes out on the right of U1 to a short wide pour at the top pad of **L1**, which sits below/right of U1. **C4 / R4 (BST)** sit just right of U1 at the top.
- **VOUT** is L1's lower pad, running down. **C6, C7, C8** sit left of L1's lower half, returning to the same GND via field.
- **R1–R3 and R5–R7, C9** (EN/RT/SS and FB parts) sit in a row **above** U1, away from L1 and SW.
- A **separate thin VOUT sense trace** leaves the VOUT pour (via at the right) for the FB divider.

**Bottom (Fig. 12-3):**
- A **solid GND plane** stitched by the same via fields.
- **One trace**: the VOUT-sense route (via near R5 at the top right → down the right side → via at the VOUT area). It runs on the bottom under the ground plane's shadow, away from SW.

## 4. Thermal, vias and land pattern

- **No exposed pad.** Heat leaves through the pins, mostly GND (4) and VIN (3), into the copper. RθJA is 112.2 °C/W (JEDEC) and **60.2 °C/W on TI's EVM (2-layer, 2 oz)** (p.6, §8.4 Thermal Information, notes 2–3). TI warns that the JEDEC RθJA must not be used for design (p.33, §10.2.2.11).
- RθJA depends on PCB area, copper heat-sink area and the **number of thermal vias under the package** (p.33–34, §10.2.2.11).
- **Land pattern DRL0008A** (p.49): pads **8 × 0.67 × 0.3 mm**, pitch 0.5 mm, row span (1.48) mm. **NSMD is preferred**, with 0.05 mm max mask expansion. Note 7: the pattern aligns to IPC-610 bottom-termination inspection.
- Stencil (p.50): **0.1 mm thick**, apertures 1:1 (8 × 0.67 × 0.3).
- No via size or count is given.

## 6. Spec cross-check (spec_power.md §6 "Buck (U951)")

- "C953/C951 across VIN (3)–GND (4) within 1 mm" is **consistent** with p.40 bullets 2–3. The 1 mm is a project target.
- "SW node small" and "FB parts at pin 8, away from SW and L951" are **consistent** with bullets 4 and 6.
- **Not in the spec but in TI**:
  - same-layer placement of all power parts;
  - > 10 mil widths for BST/FB/SS/RT traces;
  - a separate VOUT sense trace to the top FB resistor;
  - TI's 2 oz / 2-layer thermal basis (the EVM's 60.2 °C/W will not transfer directly). At 3.3 V and ≤ 1 A the loss is small, so this is low risk.
