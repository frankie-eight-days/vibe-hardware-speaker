## 2. Layout guidance in the datasheet

**The CH224 datasheet has no PCB-layout section.** The only placement or connection requirements are in the pin table and the reference schematics:

1. **GND / pin 0 (exposed pad):** "public ground, heat-dissipation base plate" (公共接地端，散热底板) (p.3, Table 4-1). Solder it to GND.
2. **VHV (pin 1):** working supply input; **"external 1 µF capacitor to ground (mind the voltage rating)"** (外接 1uF 对地电容（注意耐压）) (p.3, Table 4-1).
3. **VBUS (pin 8):** voltage-sense input, **must be shorted to VHV** (需要短接至 VHV) (p.3, Table 4-1).
4. **DP/DM (4/5), CC1/CC2 (7/6):** connect directly to the Type-C receptacle (Fig. §6.1.1, p.8). No series parts are shown.
5. CFG1 (9): Rset to GND for single-resistor mode. CFG2/SCL (2) and CFG3/SDA (3) have internal pull-ups; leave them open if I2C is unused (p.8, §6.1.1).
6. Reference schematic §6.1.1 (p.8): the **1 µF sits between the VHV node and GND, drawn at the chip**. No other passives are needed.

## 4. Package, land pattern, thermal

- **DFN10 (§9.2, p.15):**
  - Body 2.0 ± 0.1 mm square; pitch **0.4 mm**.
  - Lead width **0.2 ± 0.05**, lead length **0.3 ± 0.05**.
  - **Exposed pad 1.5 ± 0.2 × 0.9 ± 0.15 mm** with a chamfered pin-1 corner. Height 0.75 ± 0.05.
  - Pin-centre spacing is nominal; other dimensions are ±0.2 mm unless stated (p.15).
- **No land pattern, via or thermal recommendation is given.** The project uses TI's DSQ0010A WSON-10 pattern (EP 0.9 × 1.5) as a geometric match (spec_power §0).
- Thermal: no RθJA is given. CH224Q dissipation is negligible (a controller; VBUS power does not pass through it).

## 5. Applied to this board (U603)

| Check | Value / rule | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| C604 (1 µF, 50 V) | At pin 1 (VHV), GND side straight to the EP/GND copper; a ≥ 35 V part (20 V PD contracts) | p.3 Table 4-1; spec choice 50 V |
| VBUS (8) ↔ VHV (1) | Short together at the IC | p.3 |
| EP | Soldered to GND. The spec asks for **≥ 2 vias** into L2 under the 1.5 × 0.9 EP. This is not a WCH requirement, but it is reasonable for a 0.4 mm DFN ground return. Tent or plug the in-pad vias (JLC via-in-pad) to avoid solder wicking on such a small pad. | p.15; project rule |
| CC1/CC2/DP/DM | Short direct runs from J601, after U602/U601 ESD | p.8 |
| Q601/Q602 (I2C isolators) | Next to U603 pins 2/3 | spec §6 |
| Footprint | WSON-10 DSQ0010A rotated: check pin-1 orientation and that the EP chamfer matches the CH224Q bottom view (pin 1 at the bottom right in the bottom view, p.15) | p.15 |
