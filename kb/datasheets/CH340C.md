## 2. Layout guidelines in the datasheet

1. **PCB rules (Doc A p.10, §7.1, last paragraph):**
   - (a) **put the decoupling caps (C8 on V3, C9 on VCC) as close as possible to their CH340 pins**;
   - (b) **route D+ and D− close together and parallel, with ground traces or copper on both sides** to reduce interference;
   - (c) keep XI/XO traces short. This is not applicable to the CH340C, which has no crystal;
   - (d) surround the relevant parts with ground traces or copper pour to reduce HF interference.
2. **VCC needs an external 0.1 µF decoupling cap** (p.3, §4 pin table).
3. **3.3 V operation: tie V3 to VCC** and feed 3.3 V to both. Other circuits connected to the CH340 must not exceed 3.3 V. At 5 V, V3 gets its own 0.1 µF instead (p.5, §5.1).
4. **UD+ and UD− connect directly to the USB bus.** The chip has a built-in pull-up, and **no additional series resistors** should be added (p.5, §5.1; p.2 note on the internal USB transceiver).
5. GND (pin 1) connects directly to the USB bus ground (p.3, §4).
6. If the product has its own supply, power the CH340 from that supply, not from USB VBUS, to avoid I/O back-current. If both are needed, join the supplies through about 1 Ω and connect the grounds directly (p.10, §7.1).

## 3. Example layout

No PCB example is given. §7.1 (p.10) describes the reference schematic: C8 0.1 µF on V3, C9 0.1 µF on VCC, crystal parts unused on the CH340C.

## 4. Thermal / land pattern

SOP-16 150 mil (p.2, §3). No land-pattern, thermal-pad or via recommendation is given. Use the standard IPC SOIC-16 (KiCad `SOIC-16_3.9x9.9mm_P1.27mm`). There are no thermal concerns (supply current is a few mA).

## 6. Spec cross-check (spec_signal.md §2.6, §2.2, §8)

- "Place C201 at pin 16": **consistent** (p.3, p.10).
- "Route USB_DP/USB_DN as a 90 Ω differential pair … over unbroken GND": consistent with WCH's "parallel, ground on both sides" (p.10). The 90 Ω value comes from USB 2.0, not WCH.
- §8 "§5.1 V3/VCC at 3.3 V, UD± direct": **correct** (p.5).
- §2.2 should show UD± with **no series resistors** (WCH p.5 explicit recommendation). This was not checked here; schematic-review should confirm.
