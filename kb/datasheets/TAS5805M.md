## 2. Layout guidelines (Doc A §9.1, p.89–91; plus §7.3.1 p.30 and §8.1 p.77–78)

1. **Start from the §9.2 example layouts.** They are TI's "exemplary baseline". Modify only slightly; to trade thermal against EMI, use more contiguous copper, or inner-layer routing with a via picket-fence (p.89, §9.1.1).
2. **PVDD small bypass caps as close to the PVDD pins as possible.** Distance raises EMI and **can cause ringing on the outputs that exceeds the absolute-maximum ratings and damages the device**. The caps "must be no further away from their associated PVDD pins than what is shown in the example layouts in Section 9.2" (p.89, §9.1.2).
3. Decouple PVDD with **low-ESR/low-ESL bulk > 22 µF**. For HF, put **1 µF or 0.1 µF caps as close as possible to the PVDD pins** (p.78, §8.1.3).
4. Put **all decoupling (DVDD, AVDD, GVDD, PVDD) as close as possible to its pins**, avoiding inductance between supply pins and caps. Connect a small ceramic **from each BST_x to its OUT_x** (p.30, §7.3.1). Use **0.22 µF** per output (p.77, §8.1.1).
5. **Thermal, device and copper** (p.89–90, §9.1.3.1):
   - Keep other heat sources away, including above and below in the end product.
   - Use a higher layer count so that traces do not break the top and bottom contiguous copper.
   - **Place the device away from the PCB edge** so heat spreads on all four sides.
   - **Do not cut the heat flow with traces or via strings.** Route traces and line up via columns **perpendicular to the device**.
   - **Orient passives narrow end toward the device**, unless copper can flow between their pads.
   - Keep a **contiguous ground plane from the GND pins** out to the surrounding area.
6. **Thermal pad** no smaller than the package-addendum pad (p.90, §9.1.3.2.1).
7. **Thermal vias** (p.90, §9.1.3.2.1):
   - **Remove thermal reliefs** on thermal vias.
   - Filled vias are best; plain plated vias are acceptable.
   - Small diameter with minimal annular ring. The text reads "**The diameter of the drull must be 8 mm or less**", which is an obvious typo, most likely 8 **mil** (0.2 mm), matching the 0.2 mm vias in the land pattern (p.100).
   - Minimise the via-barrel-to-plane clearance, set by the voltages present.
   - **Arrange vias in columns radiating outward** from the device.
   - **Don't let vias choke the power current path in inner planes.** Remove the farthest vias if needed.
8. **Stencil:** use an **aperture array on the thermal pad** whose total area is **70–80 % of the pad** (p.90–91, §9.1.3.2.2).

## 3. Example layouts

### Doc A Figure 9-1 (stereo BTL, ferrite-bead filter) and Figure 9-2 (stereo BTL, inductor filter), p.91, §9.2

**Fig. 9-2 is the relevant one for this board (LC filter).**
- The IC sits on the top layer with pins along its long sides. The digital/I2S fan-out goes left. The output pins (right side) fan out on wide top-layer traces to **four shielded inductors in a vertical column** at the right.
- **Small PVDD caps sit at both ends of the right-hand pin row**, adjacent to pins 15/16 and 27/28. Each has a cluster of **GND vias** (about 2 × 3 dots) beside it.
- **BST caps** are 0402/0603 parts **between the IC's right pin row and the inductors**, on the top layer, connected pin-to-pin with no vias.
- **Output caps** (small, one per leg) sit next to the connector terminals beyond the inductors, each with a "+"-shaped GND-via cluster.
- **Bottom layer (Fig. 9-2):**
  - **Two large electrolytic PVDD bulk caps** sit on the bottom, under the inductor area.
  - A **3-column via array under the PowerPAD** (about 3 × 7 visible) ties the pad to the bottom GND copper.
  - The bottom is otherwise a mostly unbroken GND pour with a few signal traces at the edges.
- Fig. 9-1 (ferrite-bead version): same IC area. Ferrite beads and small caps replace the inductors and sit close to the IC. The same 3-column thermal-via array shows on the bottom.

### Doc B SLOU510 Figure 7 (top overlay, p.6) and Figure 8 (bottom overlay, p.7)

There are two amps. **U1 uses the ferrite-bead filter** (L1–L4 = 300 Ω NFZ2MSM301S + C1/C10/C11/C18 = 1000 pF). **U2 uses the inductor filter** (L5–L8 = **4.7 µH** Murata/Toko 1255AY, 6.3 × 4.5 mm + C23/C32/C36/C42 = **0.68 µF 0805**) (BOM p.7–10).

U2 placement, read from the overlay; distances are estimated from the drawing scale using the 9.7 mm package length, so ±0.5 mm:
- **PVDD pins 27/28 end:** C26, C27 (22 µF 0805) and **C28 (0.1 µF 0402)**. C28 is at the pin row end, about **≤ 1 mm** from the PVDD pins. C26/C27 are stacked beyond it, about **2–3 mm** away. A via cluster (GND) is next to them.
- **PVDD pins 15/16 end:** mirror image with **C29 (0.1 µF 0402)** and C30, C31 (22 µF 0805), plus a via cluster.
- **BST caps C35, C38, C40, C41 (0.22 µF 0603):** between the right pin row and the inductors. C35/C40 are next to the IC; C38/C41 are in the centre column between the OUT_A− and OUT_B− traces.
- **Inductors L5, L6, L8 (+L7):** in a column about 5–8 mm right of the IC, each fed by a short top trace from its OUT pin.
- **Output caps C23/C32 and C42/C36:** right at the speaker connector J6/J8 terminals.
- **DVDD:** C33 (4.7 µF) and C34 (0.1 µF) at the upper left by pin 2. **AVDD/VR_DIG:** C39 and C37 (1 µF) on the left side.
- **Bottom (Fig. 8):** **C24, C25 (390 µF, 35 V, 10 × 10)** PVDD bulk on the bottom, beside PVDD1/PVDD2 jacks. Under U1 and U2 there are dense via fields (dotted arrays visible on the bottom overlay).

## 4. Thermal pad, vias and land pattern

- **RθJA:** 29.1 °C/W on a JEDEC 4-layer board; **24 °C/W on the TAS5805MEVM-4 4-layer board**. ψJB 8.1 / 7.6 °C/W, RθJC(bot) 2.2 °C/W (p.7, §6.4).
- **Land pattern PWP0028M** (p.100):
  - Signal pads **28 × 1.5 × 0.45 mm**, pitch 0.65, row span (5.8) mm. NSMD preferred.
  - **Thermal copper (3.4) × (9.7) mm**, partly under the mask; Note 9 says the size may vary for creepage.
  - **Solder-mask opening (SMD) (3.1) × (4.05) mm.**
  - **24 vias Ø0.2 mm** in **3 columns × 8 rows, 1.2 mm pitch**.
  - Note 10: vias are optional; vias under paste should be **filled, plugged or tented**.
- **Stencil** (p.101): pad paste opening **3.10 × 4.05 at 0.125 mm stencil** (3.47 × 4.53 at 0.1; 2.83 × 3.70 at 0.15; 2.62 × 3.42 at 0.175).
  - The addendum shows a single opening sized to the mask window, which is ≈ 100 % of the exposed pad at 0.125 mm.
  - The §9.1.3.2.2 text asks for a **70–80 % aperture array**. These two instructions conflict; the text (70–80 %) is the layout guidance, and the addendum is the generic package drawing.

## 6. Spec cross-check (spec_signal.md §3.6 and §8)

- "TI §9.1.2 warns that distant PVDD caps cause output ringing beyond the absolute-maximum ratings": **correct** (p.89).
- "100 nF ≤ 1 mm from pins 15/16 and 27/28" and "10 µF within 3 mm": **consistent** with the EVM placement (SLOU510 Fig. 7, estimated). TI itself gives no mm figure; it says "no further than §9.2".
- "PowerPAD … 24 vias (3 × 8 at 1.2 mm pitch, 0.2 mm drill, **0.5 mm pad**)": the grid and drill **match p.100**. The **0.5 mm via pad is not in TI's drawing**; it comes from the KiCad footprint.
- "Fallback 0.3/0.6 mm vias (≥ 3 × 7)": this goes against TI's small-via guidance (8 mil, read as mil). It is acceptable as a deliberate trade-off, but say so in the review.
- "Paste coverage 70–80 % (§9.1.3.2)": **correct** (p.90–91, §9.1.3.2.2). The addendum stencil (p.101) shows a single opening; see §4.
- "RθJA ≈ 24 °C/W on the TI 4-layer EVM": **correct** (p.7, §6.4, TAS5805MEVM-4 column).
- "Route OUT traces ≥ 1.0 mm (≥ 4 A peak)": this is a project number, not TI's.
- **Missing from the spec**:
  - **keep the amp away from the board edge** (§9.1.3.1). The spec says to put the speaker connectors at the board edge, which is fine as long as the IC itself has copper on all sides;
  - **via columns perpendicular / radial, no via strings across the heat path**;
  - **narrow end of passives toward the IC**.
- §8 Sources cites "SLOU510 … Fig. 5 (amp schematic … 4.7 µH + 0.68 µF at 768 kHz)": the BOM confirms L5–L8 4.7 µH 1255AY and 0.68 µF on the U2 channel. U1 on the same EVM uses ferrite beads.
