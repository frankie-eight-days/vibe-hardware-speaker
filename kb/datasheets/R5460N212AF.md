## 2. Layout-relevant guidance (the datasheet has no layout section)

The only layout-related text is in "Typical Application and Technical Notes" (p.18):
1. **R1 (VDD series, 330 Ω typ.) and C1 (VDD–VSS, 0.1 µF typ.)** stabilise the IC supply, as do **R2/C2** on VC. **R1, R2 < 1 kΩ** recommended. **C1, C2 ≥ 0.01 µF** (p.18). The schematic shows 0.1 µF.
2. **C3 ≥ 0.01 µF must be mounted** "to stabilize the operation of the IC" (p.18). In the p.18 schematic, C3 connects the cell-negative/VSS node to the pack-negative/V− node (R3 side), i.e. **across the series FET pair**, right at the IC.
3. **R1 + R3 ≥ 1 kΩ, R3 ≤ 3 kΩ** (V− sense resistor) (p.18).
4. "This circuit performance **largely depends on the PCB layout** and external components; full evaluation is necessary" (p.18).
5. During a pack short, large current flows through the FETs for the short-circuit delay time. **Select FETs (and therefore copper) for that current** (p.18).
6. Absolute maximum **PD 150 mW** (p.5). This is irrelevant thermally at µA supply current.

## 3. Example layout

None. Only the typical application schematic (p.18) is given: R1/C1 on VDD, R2/C2 on VC, C3, R3 on V−, and two external N-FETs (discharge on DOUT, charge on COUT) in the pack negative path.

SOT-23-6 package; no land pattern, pad or via recommendation in the datasheet. Use standard SOT-23-6.

- Spec §3.1 "C803 10 nF VSS–V− (C3, 'must mount ≥ 0.01 µF')" and "R803 1.0 kΩ (R1 + R3 ≥ 1 k, R3 ≤ 3 k)": **consistent** with p.18.
