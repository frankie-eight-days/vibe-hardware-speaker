## 2. Layout guidelines

### 2a. BQ25798 SLUSDV2C §8.4.1 (p.139). TI calls this a priority list: "Layout of the PCB according to this specific order is essential."

1. Put **0.1 µF small caps (0402 or 0201) on the same layer as the IC, "on top of the IC"**, with short traces to PMID–GND and SYS–GND. Ideally put at least one of the 10 µF bulk caps in parallel with each. Connect the remaining bulk caps to PMID, SYS and GND with low impedance: same layer as the IC with wide traces, and/or multiple vias (p.139, §8.4.1 item 1).
2. Because of item 1, **route the inductor connections from SW1 and SW2 through multiple vias under the IC** to inner- or bottom-layer traces that reach the inductor:
   - Keep that copper **minimal in area** but wide enough for the inductor current.
   - Minimise parasitic capacitance from it to other traces and planes.
   - **The BTST caps may use vias on both sides** (p.139, §8.4.1 item 2).
3. Put the **REGN cap close to REGN** with a short trace. Its ground goes through **at least 2 vias** to the internal ground plane or pour, which has multiple vias back to IC GND (p.139, item 3).
4. Put a **0.1 µF 0402/0201 close to VBUS**; the 10 µF bulk may sit further away. Cap grounds go through **at least 2 vias** to the internal ground, which has multiple vias back to IC GND (p.139, item 4).
5. Put the **BAT caps close to BAT and GND** (p.139, item 5).
6. Size via diameter and count for each current path (p.139, item 6).
7. Route **BATP and TS away from SW1/SW2** (p.139, item 7).

Figure 8-20 (p.139) shows the high-frequency current loops of the buck-boost: the PMID cap ↔ Q1/Q2 loop (buck side) and the SYS cap ↔ Q3/Q4 loop (boost side).

### 2b. BQ25792 ZHCSMD3C §12.1 (p.135), English text

1. **SYS caps** as close as possible to SYS and GND, with a 0.1 µF 0402/0201 closer than the 10 µF. Tie grounds to IC GND with short copper or a GND plane (p.135, item 1).
2. Same for the **PMID caps** (item 2).
3. Same for the **VBUS caps** (item 3).
4. **The SYS/PMID/VBUS → 0.1 µF connection must be on the top layer, and so must its return to GND.** Keep the whole loop as small as possible (item 4).
5. Put the inductor terminals as close as possible to SW1 and SW2. Use minimum copper area that still carries the current, and minimise parasitic capacitance (item 5).
6. BAT caps close to BAT and GND, and VBUS caps close to VBUS and GND (item 6).
7. **REGN and bootstrap caps next to the IC** with the shortest trace (item 7).
8. **"Ensure that there are sufficient thermal vias directly under the power MOSFETs, connecting to copper on other layers"** (item 8).
9. Via size and count enough for the current path (item 9).
10. Route **BATP away from SW1/SW2** (item 10).

### 2c. Differences between 2a and 2b

- Doc A adds SW1/SW2-through-vias-under-the-IC, the "≥ 2 vias" for the REGN and VBUS cap grounds, and TS routing.
- Doc B has the explicit thermal-via item (8) and the "top layer, including return" item (4).

### 2d. EVM guide SLUUCB5E §3 "PCB Layout Guidelines" (p.14), in placement priority order

1. **HF decoupling for PMID and SYS** (the text names "C3 and C18"; see the discrepancy note below) as close as possible to their pins and the GND pin, **on the IC layer, no vias** (p.14, item 1).
2. **PMID and SYS bulk caps** also close and on the IC layer with **no vias** (item 2).
3. **REGN cap (C35) and BTST caps (C6, C8)** as close as possible, **using vias on only one side of each part if necessary** (item 3).
4. **VBUS and BAT HF caps** close, with **at least 2 vias per terminal** if vias are needed (item 4).
5. **VBUS and BAT bulk caps** close, with ≥ 2 vias per terminal if needed (item 5).
6. **Inductor close to SW1/SW2.** Multiple vias are acceptable here, because they add only small inductance in series with an inductor (item 6).
7. The EVM has AGND and PGND planes joined near the GND pin, but **two grounds are not required**. Return ILIM, TS and similar setting parts to a common ground **away from the high-current switching return paths** (item 7).

Discrepancies inside TI's documents:
- EVM item 3 says to use vias on **one** side of the BTST caps only. SLUSDV2C item 2 allows vias on **both** sides, and Fig. 8-21 puts the BTST caps on the bottom layer.
- EVM item 1 names **C3** as the PMID HF cap. The EVM BOM lists C3 as an **unpopulated 1 µF 0805** (p.24). The fitted 0402 0.1 µF caps next to U1 are **C32 and C18** (BOM p.22, "C13, C14, C15, C18, C32 0.1 µF 0402"; Fig. 4-1 p.15).

## 3. Example layouts

### Figure 8-21, BQ25798 SLUSDV2C p.140 (identical to Figure 12-2 in ZHCSMD3C p.136)

The figure has two panels: the left panel has top copper pours removed, the right panel shows them. The legend is red = top, light blue = mid layer, dark blue = bottom. Labels are "C1" (SYS side), "C2" (PMID side), "C3" (VBUS), "C6" (BAT), "C7" (REGN and BTST) and "L5" (inductor).
- **Two 0402 0.1 µF caps sit directly above the IC on the top layer**, one across PMID–GND ("C2", left of centre) and one across SYS–GND ("C1", right of centre), each touching the pins. Large bulk caps sit just beyond them: two "C2" on PMID (left) and two "C1" on SYS (right).
- **SW1 and SW2 leave the IC through vias under the package.** They run on the **mid layer** (light blue) straight up to the inductor L5 pads, which sit above the cap row. At the inductor end each net has about **3 vias in a column** (shown on the right panel as three vias each on the SW1 and SW2 pours).
- Between the top-layer 0.1 µF caps and the inductor, the top-layer area is a **PGND pour**.
- **BTST caps "C7"** (yellow tags) sit on the **bottom layer** (dark blue) directly under the IC edges, reached through the SW vias.
- The **REGN cap "C7"** sits at the lower left on the top layer. The note says it has at least **2 GND vias** (not drawn).
- The **VBUS small cap "C3"** sits at the left, next to the VBUS pins, with bulk "C3" beyond it. Its ground has ≥ 2 vias (note on p.140).
- **BAT caps "C6"** (two) sit to the right of the IC at BAT, returning to a PGND pour.
- The right panel shows top-layer pour names: PMID (left of the IC), SYS (right), VBUS (lower left), BAT (lower right) and PGND (top and bottom). Many GND stitching vias surround the IC.

### EVM BMS034, SLUUCB5E §4.1 (Fig. 4-1 top p.15, Fig. 4-2 signal layer 1 p.16, Fig. 4-3 signal layer 2 p.17, Fig. 4-4 bottom p.18)

This is a 4-layer board.
- **Top (Fig. 4-1):**
  - U1 sits in the middle. **C32 and C18 (0.1 µF 0402)** sit side by side directly above U1, between U1 and the inductor.
  - **L2 (1 µH SPM6530, fitted)** sits above them. L1 (2.5 × 2 mm) is an unfitted alternative footprint.
  - PMID bulk **C29, C30, C31 (10 µF 0805)** are to the upper left, and SYS bulk **C19, C20, C21** to the upper right. Both groups are in rows on the IC layer, flanking the 0402s.
  - VBUS bulk C11, C12 plus 0.1 µF C13 are to the left. **REGN C35** is at the lower left of U1.
  - BAT caps C37–C40 (C38/C39 10 µF fitted) are to the right. C16/C17 (DNP) are below.
- **Bottom (Fig. 4-4):** **C6 and C8 (BTST, 47 nF 0402)** sit on the bottom layer directly under the IC, with C14/C15 nearby. This matches Fig. 8-21.
- **Inner layers (Figs. 4-2, 4-3):** mostly solid planes, with via fields around U1 and the inductor. The 90 dpi render does not resolve trace-level detail.

## 4. Thermal, vias and land pattern

- **No exposed thermal pad.** Heat leaves through the elongated power pads. RθJA 44.2, RθJB 9.7, ΨJB 9.7 °C/W (Doc A p.8, Doc B p.10, Thermal Information).
- **Land pattern RQM0029A** (Doc A p.149, Doc B p.143):
  - **33 pads 0.2 mm wide.** The power pads at the PMID–SYS edge (pins 25–29) are elongated (dimension "4X (1)").
  - Pitch is 0.4–0.45 mm (see the 2X dimension ladders).
  - **Non-solder-mask-defined pads are preferred.**
  - Note 3 points to SLUA271.
- Thermal vias "directly under the power MOSFETs" (Doc B item 8). Fig. 8-21 shows small vias in the **space between the pad rows under the IC**, not inside the 0.2 mm pads.
- No via drill size is given in any of the three documents. Item 6 / item 9 say only that via size and count must suit the current.

## 6. Spec cross-check (spec_power.md §6 "Charger (U701)")

- "Follow the TI EVM (**SLUUCB5E §4**)": the layout *guidelines* are **§3 (p.14)**; §4.1 holds the layer plots. Cite both.
- "PMID caps (C707 first, then C704–C706) on the same layer" and "SYS caps (C716 first)" are **consistent** with Doc A item 1 and EVM items 1–2.
- "BTST caps go within 1 mm of their pins" is **not a TI number**. TI's own example puts them on the bottom layer through the SW vias (Fig. 8-21), which is also acceptable.
- "GND pin 27 gets a via array into the inner GND plane" is **consistent** with Doc B item 8.
- "PROG/ILIM/TS resistors return to a quiet GND spot" is **consistent** with EVM item 7.
- **Missing from the spec**:
  - SW1/SW2 routed **through vias under the IC on an inner layer** (Doc A item 2 / Fig. 8-21). Without this, C707/C716 cannot sit tight to the pins.
  - **≥ 2 GND vias** for the REGN and VBUS caps (Doc A items 3–4).
  - "0402 or 0201" for the 0.1 µF caps. The spec uses 0603.
- spec_power §0 says "BQ25792 SLUSDG1C (LCSC copy = TI Chinese edition ZHCSMD3C)". This is correct; the English edition could not be fetched for direct confirmation (above).
